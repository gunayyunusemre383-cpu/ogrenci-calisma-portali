📚 High-Yield Terms & Concepts

Quick reference summaries extracted directly from Reader I and Lecture Slides

Week 2: Terms & Prehistory

  • Prehistory: Period before written records.
  • History: Begins with written records (~3200 BCE Egypt / Sumer).
  • Carbon Dating: Measures decay of $^{14}C$ in organic matter/fossils.
  • Stratigraphic Dating: Dating based on geological rock layers (*stratum*).
  • Oral Transmission: Distorts narratives across generations.

Week 3: Early Humans & Paleolithic

  • Bipedalism: Walking upright on two legs (defines hominids).
  • Homo habilis: First tool maker (~2.3 Mya).
  • Homo erectus: Controlled fire, first out of Africa.
  • Homo sapiens: Language ~50k ya; only surviving hominid.
  • Shanidar Cave: Neanderthal burial with flowers (early spirituality).
  • Venus of Dolní Věstonice: 25,000 BCE ceramic fertility figurine.

Week 4: Neolithic Revolution

  • Time: ~10,000–15,000 ya, coincident with end of Ice Age.
  • Chain: Agriculture $\rightarrow$ Surplus $\rightarrow$ Population $\rightarrow$ Specialization $\rightarrow$ Hierarchy.
  • Slash-and-burn: Bark girdling & burning; ash acts as fertilizer.
  • Pastoralism: Herding in arid zones; symbiotic & conflicted with farmers.
  • Niger River Valley: Urbanization & surplus WITHOUT kings or rigid hierarchy.

Week 5: Mesopotamia, Egypt & Hittites

  • Sumer: Cuneiform (~3000 BCE), wheel (~3500 BCE), Ziggurats (temples, NOT tombs).
  • Akkad: Sargon created first dynastic empire (Semitic).
  • Hammurabi: Code of 282 laws, class-scaled "eye for an eye".
  • Egypt: Predictable Nile flooding; Narmer/Menes unified ~3100 BCE; divine kingship.
  • Pyramids: Built by native peasants fulfilling corvée tax, NOT slaves.
  • Hittites: Iron Age pioneers (~1400 BCE), 3-man chariots; Treaty of Kadesh (1258 BCE).

Week 6: Indus Valley & Shang China

  • Indus (Harappa / Mohenjo-daro): Grid layout, baked bricks, world's first sewer systems, Great Bath.
  • Indus Mysteries: Undeciphered script; NO royal palaces or monuments to kings; declined by climate/river shifts (NOT Aryan conquest).
  • Shang Dynasty: Yellow River (*loess* soil); Anyang double walls; piece-mold bronze casting.
  • Oracle Bones: Divination on turtle/ox bones; Lady Fu Hao tomb (female military leader).

Week 7: The Americas

  • Olmec: "Rubber people"; 17 colossal basalt heads (individual rulers); Mesoamerican ballgame & bloodletting; cleft heads & were-jaguars.
  • Caral (Norte Chico): Oldest in Americas (~3500 BCE); pre-ceramic (NO pottery); marine diet & cotton nets; earliest quipu (khipu).
  • Chavín de Huántar: High Andes pilgrimage center; subterranean labyrinth with *Lanzón* stela; Tello Obelisk; llamas & *ch'arki* (jerky); shaman elite control.
